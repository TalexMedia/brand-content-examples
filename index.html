Proof Of Concept · Garden & Outdoor
From Scratch: The Epic Gardening Way
  • The Home Depot and the gardening creator Kevin Espiritu turn a real property into a working garden, one stage per episode: pathways, fencing, a greenhouse, an outdoor kitchen and the pergola below.
  • The pergola episode is posted as a collaboration across six channels at once, so it appears on all six rather than only Home Depot's.
  • Epic Gardening brings 4.1 million subscribers to the partnership, against Home Depot's 659,000.

Proof Of Concept · Workspaces
Make My Space Work
  • The designer Alexandra Gater rebuilds one entrepreneur's workspace in each episode, working to a budget and around how that person actually runs their business.
  • Shopify Studios makes it, and the series has its own channel separate from both Shopify's and Alexandra's, carrying 26 videos and five million views. The episode below is the most watched of them.

Alexandra Gater is a home decor expert, editor, and stylist. In each episode, she helps entrepreneurs make their home offices, workspaces and storefronts more beautiful and functional on a budget.

Thinking Outside The Box

Brands that put unusual craft and story into their advertising.

Production Value · Automotive
Between 2 Rides
  • Two versions of the same car go side by side, one left stock and one modified with parts sourced through eBay, then get tested against each other.
  • Season two ran five episodes with seven builder-creators, and eBay cut more than 200 social assets from the same footage for YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.
  • eBay counted 22.9 million views, 222,000 new subscribers and more than a million hours watched, and its channel went from sixth to first among auto-parts channels.

Format · Human Interest
Make Up Over Makeup
  • Two people with unresolved tension between them do each other's makeup while talking through what went wrong, across three episodes.
  • Every pair had already fallen out in public before filming, so the backstory was already out there before episode one. Doing someone's makeup also means sitting inches apart, which makes the conversation hard to avoid.
  • The first two pairs were TikTok creators who had documented their relationship and then split, and two castmates from Vanderpump Rules (the Bravo series set around Lisa Vanderpump's Los Angeles restaurants).
  • The third was a couple from Love Is Blind (the Netflix series where couples get engaged before meeting in person) whose wedding did not happen, and that episode did four times the numbers of the first and sixteen times the second.

Ad Deliverables · Short-Form
Oddly IKEA
  • Twenty-five minutes of ASMR, all whispering and soft tapping, shot around IKEA bedding, storage boxes and a desk lamp, made as a back-to-college ad for dorm rooms.
  • There is no story and no presenter delivering a script, just the products being handled, and it holds 3.5 million views with 7,774 comments.
